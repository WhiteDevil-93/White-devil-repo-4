---
name: tldr-reporting
description: Short answer format for finishing a task: TL;DR first, evidence second.
---

The user wants short responses even after deep work.
1. First line: the outcome in one sentence (done / blocked / needs your decision).
2. Then at most a few bullets: what changed, evidence (numbers, paths), what is not verified.
3. Last: the one decision or action needed from the user, if any.
No recap of the journey, no option surveys: give a recommendation.
